## Style

Write short, genuine first-person sentences. A good line names a concrete experience, impression or choice. Avoid hollow self-promotion, overhyped "future leader" language, fake testimonials and job titles. Make sure studying hard **and** enjoying travel both feel real. Do not add patriotic, political or other agenda framing: this is a personal site.

## Translation

English source text is embedded in `index.html`. Russian keys are in `script.js` (`t.ru`). Every `data-i18n` key needs a Russian twin. Change both, check buttons and landmarks, and preview both layouts because Russian words often expand. New long pieces of prose need fresh readability checks on 320px screens.
